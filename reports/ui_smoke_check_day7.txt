⚠️  NOTE: 50/50 prediction for Jones vs Topuria is suspect — Jones would
be a massive favorite in reality. This is a known limitation of the no-odds
model (AUC 0.605) on cross-weight-class matchups: without betting odds to
encode the mismatch, the model relies only on career stats. Topuria's
per-minute striking rate and recent form offset Jones's physical advantages
in the model's view, which is unrealistic for a HW-vs-FW hypothetical.
This is NOT a UI bug — it's a model limitation for extreme cross-division
matchups (the model was never trained on such fights).
